Verse 1
In the banyan tree by the pond 
池塘邊的榕樹上 
Chítáng biān de róngshù shàng

Cicadas are calling summer loudly
知了在聲聲叫著夏天
zhīliǎo zài shēngshēng jiàozhe xiàtiān

On the swing next to the playground
操場邊的鞦韆上
cāochǎng biān de qiūqiān shàng

Only butterflies rest on it
只有蝴蝶停在上面
zhǐyǒu húdié tíng zài shàngmiàn

Teacher's chalk on blackboard
黑板上老師的粉筆
Hēibǎn shàng lǎoshī de fěnbǐ

Still chattering away and writing non-stop
還在拼命嘰嘰喳喳寫個不停
hái zài pīnmìng jījichāchā xiě gè bù tíng

Waiting for the end of class, waiting for the end of school
等待著下課， 等待著放學
děngdàizhuó xiàkè, děngdàizhuó fàngxué

The waiting game of childhood
等待遊戲的童年
děngdài yóuxì de tóngnián

Verse 2
The commissary (welfare club) has everything
福利社里面甚麼都有 
Fúlì shè lǐmiàn shènme dōu yǒu

I just don’t have half a cent in my pocket
就是口袋里沒有半毛錢
jiùshì kǒudài lǐ méiyǒu bàn máo qián

Zhuge Shiro and the Devil Party
諸葛四郎和魔鬼黨
zhūgé sìláng hé móguǐ dǎng

Who ended up snatching the sword?
到底誰搶到那支寶劍?
dàodǐ shuí qiǎng dào nà zhī bǎojiàn?

The girl in the class next door
隔壁班的那個女孩
Gébì bān dì nàgè nǚhái

Why haven't you passed my window yet?
怎麼還沒經過我的窗前?
zěnme hái méi jīngguò wǒ de chuāng qián?

Snacks in mouth, comics in hand
嘴裡的零食 手裡的漫畫

The first love of childhood in my heart
心裡初戀的童年
xīnlǐ chūliàn de tóngnián

Verse 3
Always wait until just before bed
總是要等到睡覺前
Zǒng shì yào děngdào shuìjiào qián

Only then did I realize that I had only done a little bit of homework
才知道功課只做了一點點
cái zhīdào gōngkè zhǐ zuòle yīdiǎn diǎn

Always have to wait until after the exam
總是要等到考試以後
zǒng shì yào děngdào kǎoshì yǐhòu

Only then did I realize that I should have read the books I didn’t read
才知道該唸的書都沒有唸
cái zhīdào gāi niàn de shū dōu méiyǒu niàn

An inch of time is worth an inch in gold
一寸光陰一寸金
Yīcùn guāngyīn yīcùn jīn

The teacher once said, "an inch of gold cannot buy an inch of time"
老師說過「寸金難買寸光陰」
lǎoshī shuōguò “cùn jīn nán mǎi cùn guāngyīn”

Day after day, year after year
一天又一天，一年又一年
yītiān yòu yītiān, yī nián yòu yī nián

Confused (blurry) childhood
迷迷糊糊的童年
mímí húhú de tóngnián

Verse 4
No one knows why

No one can tell me
沒有人能夠告訴我

山裡面有沒有住著神仙

How many days

Always alone facing the sky in a daze
總是一個人面對著天空發呆

So curious, so imaginative
就這麼好奇，就這麼幻想

Such a lonely childhood
這麼孤單的童年
zhème gūdān de tóngnián
